Cooked Slowly, Served Simply

Ember & Salt began as a Sunday supper for friends around one fire. Today we still cook everythin over wood and coals, from the bread at the start to the custard at the end.

We buy from farms and fishers within a day's drive, and the menu changes with what arrives. Come hungry, share plates, and stay a while.
